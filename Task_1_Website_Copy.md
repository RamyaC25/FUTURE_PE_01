PROMPT LOGIC :

The prompts were structured using the following elements:

Business Information:
The business name, business type and location were provided
so that the AI could generate content specifically for the
selected local business.

Target Audience:
The target audience was specified as women and girls in
Shivamogga so that the content could be relevant to the
intended customers.

Services:
The salon's services were listed so the AI could create
accurate service-specific website content.

Tone:
The prompts specified a simple, friendly, professional and
persuasive tone suitable for a local business website.

Content Requirements:
Specific requirements were given for headlines,
descriptions, benefits and calls-to-action to control the
structure and usefulness of the generated output.

CTA Requirements:
Different CTA types were requested for booking, services,
bridal enquiries, self-care and contacting the salon.

Quality Constraints:
The prompts instructed the AI to avoid fake claims,
unrealistic promises and unsupported information.

 AI WORKFLOW :

Business Information

        ↓

        ↓

        ↓

        ↓

        ↓

        ↓
